# Math Teasers Bank

Pick 2–3 per session. Rotate types to keep it fresh. She found the odd-word format fun — use it regularly.

---

## Odd Word Out
One word doesn't belong. She says which one and why.

- cat, dog, **chair**, fish → chair is not an animal
- red, blue, **three**, green → three is not a colour
- apple, mango, **carrot**, banana → carrot is not a fruit
- sun, moon, **table**, star → table is not in the sky
- bus, car, **shoe**, train → shoe is not a vehicle
- swim, run, jump, **sleep** → sleep is not an action you do with your body moving (or: sleep is not exercise) — harder, use later

---

## Number Series — goes up by 1
Fill in the blank.

- 3, 4, 5, ___, 7 → **6**
- 7, 8, ___, 10, 11 → **9**
- 11, 12, 13, ___, 15 → **14**

---

## Number Series — goes up by 2
- 2, 4, ___, 8, 10 → **6** ✓ (done session 4)
- 1, 3, 5, ___, 9 → **7** (odd numbers)
- 10, 12, ___, 16, 18 → **14**

---

## Number Series — goes down by 2
- 10, 8, 6, ___, 2 → **4**
- 20, 18, 16, ___, 12 → **14**
- 15, 13, 11, ___, 7 → **9**

---

## Number Series — goes up by 3
- 3, 6, 9, ___ → **12**
- 1, 4, 7, ___, 13 → **10**
- 0, 3, 6, ___, 12 → **9**

---

## Repeating Number Pattern
The block repeats — find the next number.

- 5, 6, 7, 8, 5, 6, 7, 8, 5, 6, ___ → **7**
- 1, 2, 3, 1, 2, 3, 1, 2, ___ → **3**
- 2, 4, 2, 4, 2, ___ → **4**
- 10, 20, 30, 10, 20, 30, 10, ___ → **20** (harder — use later)

---

## Repeating Word Pattern
The block of 3 words repeats — say the missing word.

- cat, dog, fish, cat, dog, fish, cat, ___ → **dog**
- red, blue, green, red, blue, green, red, ___ → **blue**
- sun, moon, star, sun, moon, star, sun, ___ → **moon**
- big, small, big, small, big, ___ → **small** (2-word pattern — easier, use as warm-up)

---

## Notes
- Odd-word format: she enjoyed it (session 4). Lead with this each time.
- Introduce "goes down by 2" and "goes up by 3" in session 5.
- Repeating patterns (number and word) are new — start with the easier ones.
- Always have a follow-up ready if she gets it fast: "Can you make your own pattern?"
