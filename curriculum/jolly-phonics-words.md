## School Alignment

- School has covered all five vowels (a, e, i, o, u) and consonants up to **j** in alphabetical order.
- The app currently restricts **star scoring** to consonants b through j (b, c, d, f, g, h, j).
- Words using consonants beyond j (k, l, m, n, ..., z) earn **diamonds** instead of stars.
- This dual-reward system lets the child explore ahead while keeping school-aligned content as the primary track.
