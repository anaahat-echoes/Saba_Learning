# Skip Counting

## Overview

A visual ladder shows rungs from bottom to top. Lit rungs are landing spots, dim rungs are skipped. Child fills in the blanks by tapping the correct number.

## Pedagogy

- School teaches skip counting in Hindi/Urdu using a stairs metaphor.
- The visual ladder maps directly to this — no English reading required.
- 🧒 emoji marks the current position on the ladder.
- First number is always given; child fills the remaining 3.
- 4 answer choices per blank (1 correct + 3 distractors).
- Distractors are off-by-one from the correct answer to test precision.
