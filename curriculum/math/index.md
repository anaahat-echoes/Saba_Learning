## Progression Gates

- Activities 1-2 are unlocked from the start.
- Future activities will unlock based on mastery (e.g., 80%+ on Compare before Before/After).

---

## School Alignment

- School teaches math in Hindi/Urdu, not English.
- Numbers 1-50 are familiar from school.
- Skip counting (2s and 3s) is taught using the stairs metaphor (सीढ़ी छोड़कर ऊपर जाना).
- No English text in math games — all visual/numeric cues.
