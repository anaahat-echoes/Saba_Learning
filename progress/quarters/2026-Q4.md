# Q4 2026 (November–December) — Quarterly Goal

**By end of December, Saba reads a short decodable story (8–10 sentences) independently with fluency.**

This quarter consolidates everything and introduces the final major patterns: r-controlled vowels and alternative spellings. The goal shifts from decoding to reading.

---

## Monthly breakdown

| Month | Focus | Gate |
|-------|-------|------|
| November | R-controlled vowels (ar, or, ur/er/ir) + alternative spellings (/eɪ/ = ai/ay/a-e) | Gate 10: reads r-controlled words and chooses correct spelling alternative |
| December | Fluency — ORT/Biff Chip Level 1–2 books, 80+ HFW | Gate 11: reads a full short book with expression, ≤2 prompts total |

## Quarter-end gate

- [ ] Reads a complete ORT Level 1 or equivalent book independently
- [ ] 80+ HFW confirmed in context
- [ ] Reading feels like reading — not decoding word by word

## Note on younger brothers
By end of Q4, the full teaching arc from zero to early fluency will be documented. The same plan, same assets, same session format applies — starting from the Q2 quarterly goal.
