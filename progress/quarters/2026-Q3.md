# Q3 2026 (August–October) — Quarterly Goal

**By end of October, Saba reads a decodable book page independently using digraphs, magic E, and first vowel digraphs.**

This quarter cracks the patterns that unlock the majority of English words — and flips ~50 HFW from "sight words to memorise" into "words she can decode."

---

## Monthly breakdown

| Month | Focus | Gate |
|-------|-------|------|
| August | Consonant digraphs: sh, ch, th, wh, ng | Gate 7: reads 5 digraph words per family independently |
| September | Magic E (a-e, i-e, o-e, u-e) + ee, oo | Gate 8: reads magic E words and distinguishes short vs long vowel |
| October | Vowel digraphs: ai/ay, oa, ou/ow, oi/oy | Gate 9: reads mixed vowel digraph words independently |
