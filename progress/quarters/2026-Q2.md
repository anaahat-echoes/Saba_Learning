# Q2 2026 (May–July) — Quarterly Goal

**By end of July, Saba reads any short-vowel CVC sentence aloud with no more than 1 prompted stop.**

This quarter builds the foundation everything else rests on: blending mechanics, all 5 short vowels, basic consonant clusters, and first connected reading.

---

## Monthly breakdown

| Month | Focus | Gate |
|-------|-------|------|
| May | Short A, E, I — CVC words, finger-tap blending | Gates 1–3: 6/8 CVC words per vowel |
| June | Short O, U — clusters (snap, stop) — 2-word phrases | Gates 4–5: 6/8 CVC words per vowel |
| July | Mixed-vowel sentences — first decodable book page | Gate 6: 2–3 sentence passage, ≤1 prompt |

## Quarter-end gate

- [ ] Reads a mixed-vowel sentence cold (no family scaffold)
- [ ] Reads a decodable book page with ≤1 prompt
- [ ] 30+ HFW confirmed (decodable words absorbed through blending)

## Carry forward to Q3
Digraphs (sh, ch, th, wh, ng) and magic E are the next unlock. Without them, ~50 of the 100 HFW remain opaque.
