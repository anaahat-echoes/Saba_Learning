## Lesson plan

### Vowel Sounds video — A/E/I segment (0–5 min)

Same video as sessions 1 and 2, now extended to the I segment. Play through A, E, I. Pause at I:
> *"That's our new sound today — /i/. Listen: bit, sit, pin."*

### Word Ladders — page 5, -it family (5–13 min)

Words: **sit, bit, fit, hit, kit**

> **Vocabulary first** — all may be new. Before blending, mime or show each meaning:
> - **sit** — she knows this, start here
> - **hit** — mime hitting a ball
> - **bit** — a small piece ("just a bit")
> - **fit** — it goes in properly
> - **kit** — a set of things (tool kit, first aid kit)

**I do (2 min)**
Model on **sit**:
> *"S — /s/ (tap) — I — /i/ (tap) — T — /t/ (tap). Sssiit. SIT!"*

**We do (2 min)**
Tap **bit** together.

**You do (4 min)**
She taps **fit**, **hit**, **kit** alone. Wait 5 seconds before helping.

### Three-vowel chain — oral (13–18 min)

She now has /i/ context from page 5. This is the first /e/ contrast exercise.

> *"I'll say a word. Change the vowel — tell me what you get."*

Chains: **sat → set → sit** &nbsp;|&nbsp; **pan → pen → pin** &nbsp;|&nbsp; **tan → ten → tin**

Do each chain slowly first, then pick one and repeat faster. If she confuses /e/ and /i/ — note which direction. Don't over-correct.

### Word Ladders — page 6, -in family (18–26 min)

Words: **win, pin, bin, fin**

> **Vocabulary first:**
> - **win** — she knows this
> - **pin** — the pointy thing (safety pin)
> - **bin** — rubbish bin
> - **fin** — fin of a fish (show a quick image if possible)

**You do (5 min)**
Jump straight to independent — she has the pattern from page 5.

If she finishes early: *"Can you think of another word that rhymes with pin?"*

### Vowel sort — /e/ vs /i/ pairs, oral (26–31 min)

Second /e/ contrast exercise.

Tutor says one word. She holds up one finger for /e/, two fingers for /i/.

> *"Listen carefully — pen or pin? Which vowel did you hear?"*

Pairs: **pen/pin** &nbsp;|&nbsp; **pet/pit** &nbsp;|&nbsp; **peg/pig**

If she's solid: add **net/nit, bed/bid** as stretch.

### Match Words to Pictures — worksheet (31–34 min)

Print: `sessions/2026-05-31/assets/ShortI_MatchPictures.html`

Words: **sit, pin, bin, pig, six, ink**

She reads each word, taps it, then draws a line to the matching picture. Independent — no help unless completely stuck.

Watch: does she blend without the ladder scaffold?

### Alphablocks — I episode (34–35 min)

https://youtu.be/hDvWymWpUBY

Closing treat. Let her enjoy it.
