Session #3 — Saba — 31 May 2026

Duration: 35 min  |  Format: Video call  |  Focus: Short I — A/E/I contrast built in
Session goal: Introduce /i/ with the familiar video, now to the I segment. Three-vowel chain after first ladder — contrast once she has /i/ in her ear. Two /e/ contrast exercises baked into the flow. Vocabulary pre-teaching before each ladder.

Vowel Sounds Video — A/E/I segment

0–5 min
Play
youtu.be/RUSCz41aDug — same video, now to the I segment. Pause at each vowel.
At I
"That's our new sound today — /i/. Listen: bit, sit, pin."

Word Ladders — Page 5, -it family

5–13 min
Vocab first
Before blending, check meanings. Mime or show each:
sit   hit   bit   fit   kit
sit — she knows it. hit — mime a ball. bit — a small piece. fit — it goes in. kit — a set of things (tool kit).
I do
Model on sit:
"S — /s/ (tap) — I — /i/ (tap) — T — /t/ (tap). Sssiit. SIT!"
We do
Tap bit together. Say sounds in unison, she says the whole word.
You do
She taps fit, hit, kit alone. Wait 5 seconds before helping.

Three-Vowel Chain — oral  [/e/ contrast #1]

13–18 min
How
"I'll say a word. Change the vowel — tell me what you get."
Chains
sat → set → sit  |  pan → pen → pin  |  tan → ten → tin
Do each chain slowly first, then pick one and repeat faster. Note the direction of any /e/ vs /i/ confusion — don't over-correct.

Word Ladders — Page 6, -in family

18–26 min
Vocab first
win   pin   bin   fin
win — she knows it. pin — pointy thing. bin — rubbish bin. fin — fish's fin (show image if possible).
You do
Jump straight to independent — she has the pattern from page 5.
If she finishes early: "Can you think of another word that rhymes with pin?"

Vowel Sort — /e/ vs /i/ pairs  [/e/ contrast #2]

26–31 min
How
Tutor says one word. She holds up 1 finger for /e/, 2 fingers for /i/.
"Listen carefully — pen or pin? Which vowel did you hear?"
Pairs
pen / pin  |  pet / pit  |  peg / pig
If she's solid: stretch with net/nit, bed/bid.

Match Words to Pictures — oral

31–34 min
Words
bin   pin   fin   sit   hit   kit
Show a picture on screen — she blends and says the word. If fast: reverse — you say the word, she describes the picture.
Note
If a printed short I matching sheet is found before the session, use that here instead.
