# Lesson plan format

## Structure

Plans are organised by **asset first**, steps second.

```
### [Asset name] — [pages / segment] ([time range])

**[Step label]**
...

**[Step label]**
...

### [Next asset name] — ...
```

This lets you see at a glance which document to have open and when to switch.

## Rules

- Each top-level heading in the lesson body = one asset (worksheet, video, etc.)
- Warm-ups that use a video or song get their own heading too
- Steps (I do / We do / You do) are bold sub-items under the asset heading, not separate numbered headings
- Time ranges go in the asset heading, not on each step
- "End on a win" is its own heading if it's just a recap — no asset to name

## Example

```markdown
### Word Ladders — pages 1–2 (5–20 min)

**I do (2 min)**
Model finger-tapping on cat and hat.

**We do (3 min)**
Tap bat and mat together.

**You do (5 min)**
She taps rat and sat alone.

### Match Words to Pictures — pages 1–2 (20–25 min)

**Independent (5 min)**
Tap each word, then draw a line to the picture.
```
