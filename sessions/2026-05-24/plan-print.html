Session #2 — Saba — 24 May 2026

Duration: 35 min  |  Format: Video call  |  Focus: Short E — A vs E contrast
Session goal: Give her ear a target for /e/ before blending it. Run finger-tap on -et and -en families. Close with a game and a treat.

Vowel Sounds Video — A vs E segment

0–5 min
Play
youtu.be/RUSCz41aDug — A vs E segment only. Pause after each vowel.
Ask
"What's different between these two sounds?"
"Cat — is that /a/ or /e/?"   then   "Net — is that /a/ or /e/?"
Ear-training only. No writing yet.

Word Ladders — Page 3, -et family

5–13 min
Words
net   jet   vet   pet
J and V may be unfamiliar — say the sound for her and move on. Focus is /e/ and the blend.
I do
Model on net:
"N — /n/ (tap) — E — /e/ (tap) — T — /t/ (tap). Nnn-eee-t. NET! Same /e/ from the video."
We do
Tap pet together. Say sounds in unison, she says the whole word.
You do
She taps jet and vet alone. Wait 5 seconds before helping.

Word Ladders — Page 4, -en family

13–18 min
Words
hen   pen   ten   men
All consonants likely covered. Jump straight to independent.
You do
She taps all four alone. If she finishes early:
"Can you think of another word that rhymes with hen?"

Match Words to Pictures — Page 5

18–25 min
Words
pet   net   pen   peg
Independent
Tap each word → draw the line to the picture. No help unless completely stuck.
Watch: does she blend without the ladder? Does she self-correct?
End on win
Pick her cleanest word. Ask her to say it one more time alone.

Word Chain Game — oral, no sheet

25–30 min
How
"Change the /a/ in man to /e/. What do you get?"
Chains
man → men  |  tan → ten  |  sat → set  |  pat → pet  |  pan → pen
Keep it fast. If she's stuck, give the answer and move on — momentum is the point.
