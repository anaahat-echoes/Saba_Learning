Session #1 — Saba — 17 May 2026

Duration: 25 min  |  Format: Video call  |  Focus: Short A — finger-tap blending
Session goal: Introduce finger-tap blending using the familiar short A sound as the vehicle. Informally gauge Gate 1 baseline.

Warm-up — Jolly Phonics video, /a/ segment

0–5 min
Play
youtu.be/ChqnN3cKzXQ — /a/ segment only. Pause after it.
Ask
"What sound is that?"   "Can you think of a word with that sound?"
Keep it loose — just get her talking.

Word Ladders — Pages 1 and 2 (-at and -an families)

5–20 min
I do
Model on cat and hat:
"Watch my fingers. C — /c/ (tap) — A — /a/ (tap) — T — /t/ (tap). Now: caaaaat. CAT!"
We do
Tap bat and mat together. Say sounds in unison, she says the whole word.
You do
She taps alone. Wait 5 seconds before helping.
rat   sat   pan   man   ran   fan

End on a win

20–25 min
Close
Pick the word she blended most confidently. One more time, alone.
"You just read that word all by yourself."
