# Session 4 — Short /o/ — 12 Jul 2026

**Duration:** 30–40 min (flex on her energy)
**Format:** Video call
**Focus:** Reinforce /e/ and /i/ → brain teasers → introduce Short /o/

## Session Goal
After a 5-week gap, anchor /e/ and /i/ before introducing /o/. Use brain teasers as an energy bridge. Four-vowel chains (hat→hit→hot) carry all three new sounds in one activity.

## Blocks

### Block 1 — Video: E and I segments (0–4 min)
- Play youtu.be/RUSCz41aDug — advance past A, pause at E and I
- Quick check: "What sound is this?" for each

### Block 2 — /e/ and /i/ Reinforcement (4–12 min)
**Vocab first** — mime/show any she's forgotten: pen, pin, pet, pit, peg, pig

**Three-vowel chains** (the star activity — do at least 2 rounds):
- sat → set → sit
- pan → pen → pin
- tan → ten → tin

**Vowel sort** — 1 finger for /e/, 2 fingers for /i/:
- pen / pin | pet / pit | peg / pig
- Stretch if solid: net/nit, bed/bid

### Block 3 — Math Teasers (12–15 min)
1. Number series: **2, 4, ___, 8, 10** — skip counting by 2 (continuing from last time)
2. **"Which is bigger — 47 or 74?"** — flips the digit order, trips kids up nicely
   - Follow up: "Give me a number bigger than 5 but smaller than 8."

### Block 4 — Video: O segment (15–19 min)
- Continue youtu.be/RUSCz41aDug to the O segment
- Pause: "Listen — hot, pot, cot. Hear the /o/?"
- Send Alphablocks O link on WhatsApp before class as fallback

### Block 5 — Short /o/ Word Ladder (19–28 min)
**Vocab first:**
- cot — baby's bed (show image)
- dot — a small circle
- hot — opposite of cold
- pot — she just guessed it!
- lot — a lot means many

**I do:** model **cot** — "C /k/ — O /o/ — T /t/. Kk-oo-tt. COT!"
**We do:** tap **dot** together
**You do:** she taps **hot**, **pot**, **lot** alone. Wait 5 sec before helping.

### Block 6 — Four-Vowel Chains (28–36 min)
*"I'll say a word. Change just the vowel each time."*
- hat → hit → hot
- pat → pit → pot

Do each chain slowly first, then faster. Note which vowel she confuses — /e/ still likely to creep in.

If energy is high, add:
- cat → cut (a→u preview — don't drill, just a taste)

### Block 7 — Oral Picture Match (36–40 min)
Show a picture, she blends and says the word. Words: dot, hot, pot, cot, pin, pen.
If fast: reverse — you say the word, she describes or mimes.
