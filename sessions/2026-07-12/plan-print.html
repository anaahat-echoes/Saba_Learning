Session #4 — Saba — 12 Jul 2026

Duration: 30–40 min (flex on energy)  |  Format: Video call  |  Focus: Reinforce /e/ + /i/ → introduce Short /o/
Session goal: After a 5-week gap, anchor /e/ and /i/ before introducing /o/. Brain teasers as energy bridge between the two halves. Four-vowel chains (hat→hit→hot) carry all three sounds in one activity.

Video — E and I segments

0–4 min
Play
youtu.be/RUSCz41aDug — advance past A, pause at E and I.
Audio issues? Share link on WhatsApp — she watches, you ask "what sound did you hear?"
Check
"What sound is this?" — point to E, then I. Quick recall, no pressure.

Reinforce /e/ and /i/

4–12 min
Vocab
Quick mime/check — any she's forgotten after the gap:
pen   pin   pet   pit   peg   pig
Chains
"I'll say a word. Change the vowel — tell me what you get."
sat → set → sit  |  pan → pen → pin  |  tan → ten → tin
Do each slowly, then pick one and repeat faster. Do at least 2 rounds — this is the main /e/ ear-training activity.
Sort
1 finger = /e/  |  2 fingers = /i/
pen / pin  |  pet / pit  |  peg / pig
Stretch if solid: net/nit, bed/bid.

Math Teasers ⚡

12–15 min
Teaser 1
Fill in the blank:  2, 4, ___, 8, 10
→ 6  (skip counting by 2 — continuing from last session)
Teaser 2
"Which is bigger — 47 or 74?"
→ 74  (digit order flip — she'll think hard)
Follow up if she gets it fast: "Give me a number bigger than 5 but smaller than 8."

Word Ladder — /o/ family

19–28 min
Vocab
Before blending, check meanings:
cot   dot   hot   pot   lot
cot — baby's bed (show image). dot — small circle. hot — opposite of cold. pot — she just guessed it! lot — a lot means many.
I do
Model cot:
"C — /k/ (tap) — O — /o/ (tap) — T — /t/ (tap). Kk-oo-tt. COT!"
We do
Tap dot together. Say sounds in unison, she says the whole word.
You do
She taps hot, pot, lot alone. Wait 5 seconds before helping.

Four-Vowel Chains — A / E / I / O

28–36 min
How
"I'll say a word. Change just the vowel each time — tell me what you get."
Chains
hat → hit → hot
pat → pit → pot
Do each slowly first, then faster. Note if /e/ creeps in (hit → het?) — don't over-correct, just model the right sound and move on.
If energy is high, add: hat → hit → hot → (hat again) as a loop.

Oral Picture Match

36–40 min
Words
dot   hot   pot   pin   pen   pig
Show a picture — she blends and says the word. If fast: reverse — you say the word, she mimes or describes.
